Tensile Evaluation of 3D-Printed Filaments at Varying Print Speeds

2026  |  ME103 Experimentation and Measurements

Designed and executed a full factorial experiment investigating how FDM print speed affects the tensile behavior of three common 3D printing filaments: PLA, PETG, and ASA. Fabricated 60 ASTM D638 dog bone specimens across 15 treatment combinations using a Bambu P1S printer, varying speed from 50 to 800 mm/s. Performed dimensional verification on all 60 specimens with digital calipers and stored them in airtight containers to minimize moisture absorption.

Conducted uniaxial tensile testing on a 20 lbf Instron 5500, calibrating the load cell against five known static loads and deriving a linear correction function. Converted force-displacement outputs into stress-strain curves in MATLAB, using individually measured cross-sectional areas per specimen, and performed full uncertainty propagation using root-sum-square methods.

Statistical analysis at a 5% significance level showed material choice was the dominant factor controlling both UTS and ductility, while print speed alone produced no statistically significant main effect. The material-speed interaction was significant, with PLA showing a clear decrease in ductility at higher speeds.

Material Testing, Additive Manufacturing, Data Analysis

Composite Toed Shoe Covers

2026  |  ME127 Introduction to Composite Materials

Designed a 3D-printed composite sandwich toe cover as a lightweight alternative to steel-toe boots, targeting ASTM F2413-18 compliance (2,500 lbf compression). Modeled three core geometries — square, honeycomb, and corrugated — in SolidWorks, all constrained to a constant thickness. Researched and compared TPU 95A, TPE, and PA-CF filaments across stiffness, yield strength, and cost, finding the TPU 95A / PA-CF sandwich substantially cheaper than steel at a fraction of the weight.

Ran ANSYS static structural simulations evaluating deformation and factor of safety, with the corrugated TPU 95A – PA-CF design achieving the highest FOS of 4.39. Also used ANSYS ACP to model fiber orientation effects on a traditional carbon fiber / aluminum honeycomb wet layup baseline.

Excalibur Occupant Cell Reinforcements

2024-2025  |  UC Berkeley Solar Vehicle Team

Led the structural redesign of CalSol's solar vehicle occupant cell as the project's Structural Design Lead. Inherited an existing composite structure already integrated into the chassis, and identified critical safety issues through simulation, with localized factors of safety as low as 0.4. Directed a team of five engineers in redesigning the cell to exceed a target minimum FoS of 1.4, balancing safety, weight, and manufacturability.

Simulations were conducted using ANSYS Mechanical under 5g load cases at eight critical locations, while composite behavior was modeled using ANSYS Composite PrepPost. The final reinforcement design included AL6061 aluminum ribs for side-impact resistance and a custom epoxy U-channel bonded with 3M DP460 for lightweight structural enhancement. Led material selection efforts and oversaw the full manufacturing process — machining all aluminum components via waterjet and saw cutting, and 3D printing molds for the epoxy parts. Also managed integration planning and coordination with the broader mechanical team.

SolidWorks, Ansys Mechanical, Manufacturing (Waterjet, Molds, 3D Printing)

catdotcom

2025  |  University of California Berkeley ME100

CATDOTCOM is an automated three-in-one cat toy designed to keep indoor cats active through a sequence of ESP32-controlled components. The system begins with a proximity sensor that triggers audio playback and wirelessly activates a laser module using ESP-NOW. The laser module uses a time-of-flight sensor to detect when the cat catches the laser, then moves it to a new position using two servos. After several successful interactions, a feeder module checks for an existing treat using a load cell and dispenses one if none is detected. All components are monitored and controlled via a live web interface that provides real-time status updates and remote control.
